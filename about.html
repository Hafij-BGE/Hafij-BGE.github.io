About

Working at the bench and on the computer

I started in the wet lab — virology, vaccines, molecular biology — and moved toward computation without leaving the bench behind. The two inform each other.

I'm a Master's student in Biomedical Technology at Dokuz Eylül University, İzmir. My lab background is in virology, vaccine development and molecular biology; my computational work covers deep learning, immunopeptidomics, molecular modelling and multi-omics. My current research focuses on deep learning–based prediction of protein–protein interactions.

What I care about most is research that can be checked. My recent projects are preregistered where possible, log every decision, and keep withdrawn results on the record rather than quietly removing them.
